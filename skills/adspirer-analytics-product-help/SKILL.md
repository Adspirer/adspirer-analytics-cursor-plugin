# Product help

Use references/doc-map.md to find official documentation. Fetch the relevant current page for
pricing, limits and changing capabilities; never quote a cached price.
Use get_usage_status for the user's quota and get_connections_status for their accounts and
analytics properties, not general documentation.
Distinguish the full Adspirer product from this Analytics endpoint. This connector only reads;
campaign creation and optimization are a separate Adspirer connector, and a product page does not
prove a write tool exists here.
If browsing fails, link the documentation and state the limitation rather than inventing a route or
answer. Never send users to machine-readable indexes as if they were customer pages.
